# Car Maintenance Guide

## Oil Changes
Regular oil changes are crucial for engine longevity. Most vehicles require an oil change every 3,000 to 5,000 miles, but check your owner's manual for the manufacturer's recommendation.

### Signs you need an oil change:
- Engine knocking or ticking sounds
- Dark, dirty oil on the dipstick
- Oil change light illuminated
- Reduced fuel efficiency

## Tire Maintenance
Proper tire care ensures safety and extends tire life.

### Tire Pressure
- Check pressure monthly
- Proper inflation improves gas mileage by up to 3%
- Under-inflated tires wear faster and reduce handling

### Tire Rotation
- Rotate tires every 5,000-8,000 miles
- Helps ensure even wear
- Improves traction and handling
