BRAKE SYSTEM MAINTENANCE

Brake pads and rotors should be inspected every 12,000 miles or annually, whichever comes first. Signs of brake wear include:

- Squealing or grinding noises when braking
- Vibration in the brake pedal
- Reduced stopping power
- Brake warning light illuminated

Brake fluid should be flushed every 2 years or 30,000 miles to prevent moisture buildup and corrosion.
